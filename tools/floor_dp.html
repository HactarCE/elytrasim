The DP (floordp, README-floor 6g) holds the best remaining endurance or range V on a grid over (h, vy, vz): 133 heights to 33 blocks, velocity every 0.025, 1700 sweeps. The flown policy picks, each tick, the pitch on a 0.25° grid with the largest r + V(s′) and flies it exactly from rest. Polished: that flight used as the seed of floor exit --method grad (l2:2 price, graze). Best before DP: the best of every earlier run (known-before-dp.tsv).

The comparison panel shows, for each 1° pitch, how much less r + V(s′) it offers than the pitch flown; that deficit is in ticks (endurance) or blocks (range). V itself is optimistic by several percent above y0 ≈ 17, so deficits far smaller than that are below what the grid can resolve. The field is the DP's policy only: the polished and earlier flights are fixed pitch schedules, not feedback policies, so they have no field. It is the same argmax over a 0.25° grid, computed in this page on every velocity node at exactly the height of the chosen flight at the probed tick, by a port of floordp's backup that reproduces its choice and value exactly on every traced DP tick. Each checked flight's path is drawn over it, bold within 40 ticks of the probe, with a ring at its state: white for the flight whose height the field is at, colored for the others, which are at their own heights. "fixed" holds the field at the slider's height instead. Below the map: what each flight flies at the probed tick, and what the DP policy would pick at that flight's exact state.

Flights that never land are shown for their first __TMAX__ ticks.
